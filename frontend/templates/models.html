Real-dataset results come first. The UNSW-NB15 models are trained on the dataset authors' official training set and tested on their official testing set. The data is real captured flows: real normal traffic plus attacks generated in a lab testbed. These are the numbers to report. The simulated-data models below only show that the pipeline works, and their near-perfect scores come from rule-generated data.
Offline evaluation is measured once on a held-out test set when a model is trained. Live detection statistics describe what the models predict on the current stream. They are not accuracy measurements, except for the agreement with ground-truth labels of replayed real flows.
